Information Sheet for New Employees

Welcome to Security USA. We're excited to have you join our team. Please find this list as a helpful resource to get you started.

Payroll Process

  • Payday is every Friday. Each payroll period is Monday 12am to Sunday 11:59pm. You will be paid on the Friday of the following week.
  • ✧ Payroll questions: Payroll@securityusainc.com
  • Check pickup at the NYC office: Mon–Thu 9am–5pm, Fri 7:30am–6pm.
  • Direct deposit: email a voided check or direct deposit form to Payroll@securityusainc.com. Takes 3 weeks to process.
  • Mailed checks go out on Fridays; USPS delivery typically 1–2 business days.

Time and Attendance

  • ✧ Clock in/out daily via the Ehub app or registered site phone. If you are having problems clocking in or out you may call Command Center: 212-594-4475.
  • You may also call 833-343-1773 from the registered site phone.
  • Failure to clock out or in may result in disciplinary action, including up to a termination.

Shift End — Wait for Relief

  • ✧ Remain at your post until properly relieved. If relief is more than 15 minutes late, contact the office immediately. Do not leave your site without being officially relieved. You may face immediate termination.

Lateness

  • Be at your post in full uniform at your scheduled start time. No lateness or grace periods. If you anticipate being late, contact the Command Center immediately.

Call Out

  • ✧ Call out at least 6 hours before your shift. Acceptable reasons: illness, doctor's appointment, family emergency, or bereavement. All callouts must be made by calling the Command Center — texts and emails are not permitted.

Uniform / Personal Hygiene

  • ✧ Full uniform required at all times — clean, pressed, and presentable. Proper footwear must be worn on duty. Uniform inquiries: help@securityusa.info
  • If you are about to receive a new assignment, you must make sure you have the proper uniform attire.

Drug Policy

  • ✧ Use of illegal drugs or alcohol while working or reporting to work impaired is strictly prohibited. Violations may result in termination.

Field Inspection

  • ✧ Routine inspections by Field Supervisors may include: a photo in proper uniform, review of the command logbook, and a brief electronic knowledge exam about the post duties.
Accessing Your Payroll — iSolved

Your employee email is used to set up your payroll account in iSolved for paystubs and W-2s. Watch for an activation email from Accudata@myisolved.com.

Questions: Help@securityusainc.com | 24/7 support: 212-594-4475

Sick Leave

  • Earn 1 hour of paid sick leave for every 30 hours worked. Notify the office at least 6 hours before your shift if sick. To claim: email Payroll@securityusainc.com.

Time Off Requests

  • Submit requests via the Ehub app or in person at the office. You must wait for approval.
  • 2 days or less: at least 14 days in advance. 3+ days: at least 30 days in advance.

Licensing / Registration

  • Begin renewal at least 3 months before expiration. Once renewed, provide a copy to help@securityusa.info. Assistance: help@securityusainc.com

Minor and Major Violations

  • Read and understand all violations in your onboarding documents and handbook. Any violation may result in discipline or termination.

Scheduling

  • Your most updated schedule is always on the Ehub app. Questions: call 212-594-4475 or email help@securityusa.info

Flex Guard

  • If you pick up a Flex Guard shift on Ehub, report to the NY office and you'll be assigned to a site anywhere in the 5 boroughs. Questions: Command Center 212-594-4475.
